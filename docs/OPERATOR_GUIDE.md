# CHASE HomePath — Operator Quick-Start Guide

For specialists, managers, and admins. The console lives at https://chasehomepath.com — sign in
with your staff account and you land on the console automatically (members see the member app).

## First sign-in
1. Sign in with the email and temporary password you were given.
2. The console requires two-step verification. Scan the QR code with any authenticator app
   (Google Authenticator, 1Password, Authy) and enter the 6-digit code. From then on you'll enter a
   code at each sign-in. This is mandatory — staff accounts handle member financial data.

## The five console areas
- **Clients** — your roster. Specialists see their own assigned members; managers and admins see
  everyone. Click a member for their full plan: tracks, credit summary, documents, appointments,
  and message history.
- **Team** — capacity and ratings (managers/admins). Watch for specialists near their client cap
  before onboarding assigns them more.
- **Inventory** — CHASE-owned homes and lots. Managers can retire listings.
- **Onboarding** — the intake queue (managers/admins): new members who finished qualify, booked
  consultations, and uploaded documents. Assign a specialist here; the first consultation is what
  unlocks the member's score visibility.
- **HQ Admin** — user/role administration and program configuration (admins only).

## The rules the software enforces (don't fight them, they're the law)
1. **Disputes are member-initiated.** You can flag an item for the member to look at; you cannot
   file a dispute for them. The button doesn't exist on your side on purpose.
2. **Accurate items can't be disputed.** The API returns a hard error. Never coach a member to
   dispute information they've confirmed is accurate.
3. **No outcome promises.** Never tell a member their score "will" go up or an item "will" be
   removed. Say what the process is and whose decision it is.
4. **Score stays hidden until the first consultation.** Don't read a member their score over any
   channel before that consultation is completed in the system.
5. **All member contact runs through the app.** No personal phone/email — the audit trail is a
   compliance requirement, not an option.
6. **90-day minimum before placement-ready.** The day counter is program design; don't shortcut it.

## Day-to-day flow
1. Check **Onboarding** for new intakes → assign specialist → confirm the consultation slot.
2. Run consultations from the client detail page — the credit review is there when the member's
   pull completes.
3. Work the roster: members with "Look" pills first, then upcoming appointments, then messages.
4. Keep documents moving: the prep checklist shows exactly what's missing per member.

## When something looks wrong
- Member can't sign in → they can reset from "Forgot password?" on the sign-in page themselves.
- Wrong data on a member screen → do not edit around it; flag to an admin so it's fixed at the
  source with an audit trail.
- Anything that smells like a security issue → admin + support@chasehomepath.com immediately.
